# Create: Sulfuric Resonance

## 0.6.1 — Tied Ends

**Tied Ends** expands familiar construction materials and closes gaps in processing, automation, and translations.

### Construction

- **Resonant Iron, Copper, and Gold Bars:** thin vertical bars with connected geometry, refined metal colors, and front-facing inventory icons. Available in the Construction tab.
- **Framed Ashesil:** regular and tempered blocks and panes with wider charcoal borders, a subtle diamond lattice, and connected textures. One Obsidian Fiber frames four matching glass blocks or eight panes. Tempered variants retain their blast resistance.

### Progression and processing

- **Thermal Instability** marks Thermal Matrix progression; Resonant Heat Injector follows it.
- **Batch Refining** marks Rotary Leacher progression.
- JEI and EMI show Leacher input counts, batch sizes, acid costs, processing times, and whisk speed. Displays and fluid animations use the machine's actual batch curve and tank capacity.
- Leacher Ponder information matches its operating values.

### Automation and fixes

- Mechanical Arms can deliver fuel directly to Sulfur Burners.
- Process Monitor linking checks server-side interaction permissions, including spawn protection.
- Breaking a Resonance Chamber drops its stored items.
- Rotary Leacher progress is marked for saving, and custom byproducts respect output stack limits.
- Resonant bars render with transparency and have the correct pickaxe mining tags.
- Framed glass handles inside corners, while pane end caps distinguish exposed ends from continuous runs.
- Process Monitor support remains focused on the Resonance Chamber and Rotary Leacher.

### Languages

Italian; joins English (US and UK), German, Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, and Simplified Chinese. All nine locale files contain 932 matching translation keys.

- **Java:** 21
