# Create: Sulfuric Resonance 0.7.0

**Moving With Purpose** brings reusable block transport, processing on Create belts, expanded thermal machinery, and mobile-world support to Create: Sulfuric Resonance.

Build a sulfur-powered factory, move its machines aboard trains or SABLE airships, and use block cargo to connect processing steps into a continuous production line.

## What is new in 0.7.0

### Move blocks through your factory

**Block Caddies** carry blocks along Create belts. Load one by hand or with a Use-mode Deployer, then unload or process its block cargo farther down the line. Empty Caddies can be parked on Depots. Placed Caddies can also be used decoratively to display blocks or ordinary items.

Caddies preserve the cargo's placed block state when available, including orientation and multipart shapes. Their cargo is visible in inventories and on belts, and supported block cargo keeps its shape-based selection and collision. Sneak-right-click supported chest and shulker cargo to open its stored inventory.

### Process cargo while it travels

Caddy cargo can use supported Create and CSR belt processes, including:

- Mechanical Presses and Saws
- Spouts
- Encased Fans for washing, smoking, blasting, splashing, and cooling
- Deployers
- CSR Combustion Belts
- Precision Spritzers

Caddies travel with the belt to each machine. They wait at a valid processing point, remain there through the machine's operation, and continue once processing finishes. Precision Spritzer recipes wait for their required spray contacts. Combustion Belt recipes transform the cargo while the Caddy continues moving.

### Separate cargo with the Scraper

The **Scraper** separates Caddies from belt contents: it extracts ordinary block cargo from loaded Caddies and routes it to connected storage, while empty Caddies are collected. Loaded storage cargo is preserved. Ordinary items traveling on the belt outside a Caddy pass through by design.

### Expand heat and power systems

- **Combustion Belt Goggles** show whether a belt is Unheated, Heated, Superheated, or at Combustion heat.
- **Thermal Batteries** can provide rotation to ordinary Create shafts, or run without a shaft when you want to use their heat storage on its own.
- **Rotor Weather Shrouding** protects the Molten Rotor Furnace from rain and shows runoff during storms.
- SABLE support covers thermochemical machinery, processing, controls, monitoring, cargo collision, and mobile Ember Lamp lighting. Ember Lamp brightness follows the linked Rotor Furnace heat tier.

### Build a thermochemical factory

Sulfuric Resonance adds a heat-driven production chain around the **Molten Rotor Furnace**, with machines and network blocks for burning and refining materials, producing and using Sulfuric Acid, and transmitting heat and rotation. Build with the Sulfur Burner, Catalyst Bed, Sulfuric Resonance Chamber, Rotary Leacher, Ash Ceramic Crucible, Thermal Battery, Thermal Link Drive, Thermal Relay Switch, and related gauges and monitors. JEI and EMI provide recipe lookup for supported viewers.

### Learn in game

Ponder tutorials explain Caddy loading, processing, storage, Precision Spritzer recipes, Saw and Spout use, and Scraper behavior. Additional Ponder guidance covers CSR machinery and thermal systems.

## Getting started

1. Build your sulfur processing and heat network using the Molten Rotor Furnace and related thermochemical machines.
2. Place a Block Caddy on a Create belt and load it by hand or with a Use-mode Deployer.
3. Add supported processing machines above the belt to transform the cargo as it passes.
4. Use a Scraper to separate empty Caddies from regular items at the end of a line.
5. Check the in-game Ponder entries for demonstrations and machine-specific details.

## Compatibility

- **Minecraft:** 1.21.1
- **Mod loader:** NeoForge 21.1.238 or later
- **Create:** 6.0.7 or later
- **Java:** 21
- JEI and EMI recipe displays are supported. Use either recipe viewer with your modpack.
- SABLE airship and sublevel compatibility is included; availability of SABLE depends on your installation.

Install the matching NeoForge and Create versions first, then add Sulfuric Resonance to your `mods` folder. Do not combine multiple incompatible versions of Create or the mod.
