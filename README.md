# Create: Sulfuric Resonance

**Create: Sulfuric Resonance** is an independently developed NeoForge addon for [Create](https://github.com/Creators-of-Create/Create), focused on **combustion, thermochemical heat, sulfur chemistry, industrial materials, and factory automation**.

CSR expands Create with heat-driven machinery, thermochemical transmission systems, sulfur-based processing, high-temperature manufacturing, specialized fuels, and new ways to integrate temperature into kinetic factories.

> **Beta:** Create: Sulfuric Resonance is actively developed. Core systems are playable, but recipes, balance, visuals, progression, compatibility, and internal systems may change between beta releases.

## Core Systems

### Molten Rotor Furnace

The Molten Rotor Furnace converts fuel and temperature into rotational power.

Different fuels provide different burn times and temperature capabilities, while increasing heat unlocks greater kinetic output and more advanced processing.

Thermal stages include:

* Smouldering
* Heated
* Superheated
* Combustion

The furnace supports Create-style automation through funnels, chutes, hoppers, pipes, and Mechanical Arms.

---

### Thermochemical Heat Network

Heat generated by CSR machinery can be routed through dedicated thermochemical infrastructure.

Current network components include:

* Thermochemical Shaft
* Thermochemical Conduit
* Thermochemical Gearbox
* Thermochemical Cogwheel
* Large Thermochemical Cogwheel

Thermochemical components can transport heat alongside kinetic power while respecting network distance and connection rules.

Standard Create cogwheels **do not transmit thermochemical heat**.

---

### Sulfur Burner

The Sulfur Burner provides an alternative source of thermochemical heat.

Rather than generating rotation like the Molten Rotor Furnace, it is designed specifically as a compact heat source for thermochemical infrastructure and processing systems.

A dedicated Ponder scene is included to explain its operation.

---

### Combustion Processing

CSR extends Create's processing system beyond normal Heated and Superheated recipes.

High-temperature processing includes:

* Combustion Mixing
* Ash Ceramic Crucible Basin processing
* Combustion Belt processing
* Thermally treated materials

The Combustion Belt allows items to undergo temperature-dependent processing while moving through a factory.

---

## Sulfur Industry

Sulfur acts as a core industrial resource throughout CSR.

Its uses include:

* Sulfuric Acid
* Specialized furnace fuels
* Sulfur Burner operation
* Sulfurous Fuel Compound
* Sulfur Fuel Briquettes
* Advanced machine components
* High-temperature materials
* Chemical processing

The **Brimstone Core** serves as a specialized sulfur-based progression component for advanced machinery and recipes.

---

## Industrial Materials

CSR introduces interconnected material chains designed around Create-style processing.

Major material families include:

* Ash Ceramic
* Ash Brick
* Ashesil
* Tempered Ashesil
* Acid-resistant ceramics
* Rubber
* Cinder materials
* Obsidian fibers
* Industrial filaments
* Acid-Etched Copper

### Tempered Ashesil

Tempered Ashesil is an upgraded form of Ashesil designed for hazardous industrial environments.

Both the full block and pane variants are **explosion resistant**, making them suitable for protected factory areas and machinery enclosures.

---

## Sulfur Chemistry

Sulfur chemistry includes the production and use of Sulfuric Acid.

Sulfuric Acid can:

* Be used in industrial recipes
* Process specialized materials
* Damage living entities
* Interact with environmental fluids
* Be transported and automated as a normal fluid

CSR uses common sulfur tags where possible to improve compatibility with other mods.

---

## Factory Automation

CSR is designed to integrate with Create-style factories instead of functioning as isolated machines.

Systems include:

* Thermal redstone control
* Heat-responsive machinery
* Automated fuel handling
* Fluid processing
* Mechanical Arm compatibility
* Engineer's Goggle diagnostics
* Ponder documentation
* JEI and EMI recipe integration

The Thermal Relay Switch can monitor linked furnace conditions and convert thermal states into redstone output.

---

## Ponder

Create: Sulfuric Resonance includes in-game Ponder documentation for its major systems.

Current scenes cover machinery including:

* Molten Rotor Furnace
* Thermal Relay Switch
* Perforated Spritzer
* Thermochemical Shaft
* Thermochemical Conduit
* Thermochemical Gearbox
* Combustion Belt
* Sulfur Burner

Additional scenes are added as new systems are introduced.

---

## Recipe Viewer Support

CSR provides dedicated integration for:

* **JEI**
* **EMI**

Custom recipe categories include thermal processing, Molten Rotor fuels, Combustion Mixing, and Combustion Belt recipes.

Recipe viewers are optional and are not required for CSR itself to load.

## Installation

1. Install Minecraft **1.21.1**.
2. Install NeoForge **21.1.247 or newer**.
3. Install Create **6.0.7 or newer**.
4. Place the Create: Sulfuric Resonance `.jar` into the `mods` folder.
5. Optionally install JEI or EMI for recipe viewing.
6. Launch the game.
